The Studio

Founded in London, Analogue Desk Co. is a design studio dedicated to the intersection of retro-futurism and modern IoT. We are obsessed with the tactile feedback of the past, powered by the precision of modern microcontrollers.

We don't do mass production. We operate on a Batch Drop model. Every unit is assembled, calibrated, and finished by hand in our UK workshop. We treat electronic objects not as disposable gadgets, but as functional art for your workspace or home.
